How buying works here

Four visits at most, and usually two.

Ride before you decide

Walk in with a licence and take any model out on the main road. No booking, no deposit.

Agree the build sheet

We write down the battery, motor, colour and any extras, with the on-road price next to it. Nothing is added later.

Finance and paperwork

EMI from about ₹2,500 a month. We file the registration and insurance while the vehicle is being prepared.

Delivery and handover

We open the vehicle up, show you the pack and the controller, and explain the charging routine before you leave.

Service that does not send you away.

Three free services in the first year, then about ₹500 for a full check. Battery health, controller diagnostics, brakes, tyres and a software update where the model supports it.

If your vehicle will not start, message us. For anything within Anakapalle town we collect it and bring it back.
